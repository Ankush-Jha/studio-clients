Mortgage renewal London Ontario 2027: three neighbours renew, and only one payment jumps.
Dan Balch, Licensed Mortgage Broker · Lic. M08003485 · Mortgage Architects · Brokerage Lic. #12728

Illustrative example. Three London neighbours each borrowed $500,000 over 25 years.
A signed a 5-year fixed in Feb 2022 at 2.81%.
B signed a 5-year fixed in Nov 2022 at 5.17%.
C signed a 3-year fixed in Mar 2024 at 5.43%.
(Those are Bank of Canada monthly averages for new uninsured fixed mortgages.)

All three renew in 2027. At 4.66%, the September 2026 average for a new 5-year fixed, and with the same years left:
A: $2,318 to $2,720 a month (+$402)
B: $2,957 to $2,835 (−$122)
C: $3,032 to $2,832 (−$200)

The rule behind it: keep the same years left, and your payment goes up only if you renew above the rate you signed. Rates have moved since September, so check your own number.

A smaller payment still isn't a reason to sign the first offer. On B's balance, each 0.25% is about $59 a month. Moving to another lender at renewal with the same balance and amortization doesn't need a new stress test, though the new lender still has to approve you.
