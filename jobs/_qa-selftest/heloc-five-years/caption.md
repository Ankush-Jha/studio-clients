## Caption

HELOC interest-only payments: five years in, the balance can be exactly where it started.

An FCAC survey found more than a quarter of HELOC holders mostly pay just the interest, and 62% of them expected to be paid off within five years. An interest-only payment doesn't reduce the balance, so that plan can't get there on its own.

Quick check: open your last statement. If the balance hasn't dropped, you don't have a payoff date yet.

Clearing $50,000 in five years takes about $833 a month toward the balance, plus interest. Illustrative example.
