# Voice and content rules: Orbis Mortgage Group

Status: DRAFT from Reel 01 only. Confirm with the client.

## Tone
Calm, plain-spoken, educational. Conditional language ("may", "depending on your situation", "could be worth exploring"). Never salesy, never promises.

## Script spine seen in Reel 01
1. Hook as a question the viewer recognises ("Are you just paying only the minimum payments due on your credit cards?")
2. "Here's what many people don't realize:" one idea
3. What it may mean, conditionally
4. Options, conditionally ("a consolidation loan or mortgage-based option may help")
5. Goal statement, then CTA: "DM me and let's look at a smarter repayment strategy"

## On-screen text rules
- Only restate what the speaker actually said.
- No invented numbers. Illustrative charts say ILLUSTRATION.
- End card always shows: agent licence #M17000370, brokerage licence #13476, the disclaimer, contact details.

## Spelling
Canadian English.

## Topics covered so far
Credit-card minimum payments, debt consolidation, mortgage-based repayment options.
