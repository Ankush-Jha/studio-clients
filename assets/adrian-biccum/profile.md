## 1. Design: minimalist, always
Every format follows this: reel, carousel, static, cover, story.
- **One idea per frame or slide.** One headline, one number or one object. Nothing else competes.
- **Lots of empty space.** Leave at least 40% of each frame clear. Text sits in a calm grid with wide margins. Never fill space for the sake of it.
- **Two colours plus neutrals.** Ink #111111 and paper (white or off-white) carry the design. Brand blue (#2270E1 for text and shapes; #13AFF0 only as a fill behind ink text, since it fails contrast on white) is the single accent, used once per frame on the thing that matters: the number, the CTA, the key word.
- **One typeface family**, two weights at most and three sizes per frame. Large, tight headlines; small, quiet supporting lines.
- **No decoration:** no textures, halftones, gradients, glows, stickers, emoji, icons used as filler, stock photos, clip-art houses or keys, drop shadows, or busy backgrounds. Lines and simple geometric shapes only when they carry data.
- **Motion (reels):** slow, precise moves. Cuts on the beat, type that slides or wipes in, numbers that count up. No shakes, glitches, particle effects or fast flashing.
- **Style choices:**
  - Stills: `bold-type` (his roster style) done in its quietest form, paper ground with ink type and one accent band at most, not field-flipping every slide. Or `bento` with very few tiles.
  - Reels (opuscar): `swiss-motion`, `one-line`, `pictogram-motion`, `white-model`, `dark-keynote`.
  - **Avoid:** riso, risograph, halftone-dossier, memphis, pop-art, collage and every other textured or loud style. This overrides his roster secondary `riso`.
  - Variety still applies. Rotate inside the minimal styles; don't repeat his last piece's device.
- **Footer:** use his supplied footer image as-is, on a clean band, with the compliance line.

## 2. Content: heavy sales, sales-oriented
Every piece exists to get a call booked or an application started. It is not general education.
- **Lead with the offer or the outcome**, not a lesson. Lines like "Bank said no? Private money can still close in days" beat "What is a private mortgage?".
- **Hook in the first second:** a direct question to a specific buyer, or one hard number, on screen and in the voice at once. 
- **Structure:** problem the viewer has now → what Adrian does about it → proof (15+ years, 30+ five-star reviews, a worked example labelled illustrative) → **one strong CTA**.
- **CTA every time, said and shown:** "Book a 15-minute call: link in bio", "DM 'PRIVATE' to see if you qualify", or "Call 905-730-4446". Use exactly one per piece. Put it on the final frame or slide and in the first line of the caption.
- **Tone:** confident, direct, short sentences, speaks to "you". It sounds like a broker who closes deals, not a teacher. Keep it upbeat and decisive.
- **Real urgency only:** tie it to real dates (renewal dates, a closing deadline, a power-of-sale timeline, a rate-decision date). Never invent scarcity ("only 3 spots left") or deadlines.
- **Captions:** first line is the hook plus CTA, then 2–4 short lines, the compliance line, and ≤5 hashtags (e.g. #privatemortgage #hamiltonmortgage).

## 3. Topic focus: private mortgages
Private lending is his main content lane. Other services (renewals, refinance, self-employed, first-time buyers) appear only when framed as a route to or from private lending, or when the brief asks for them.

Topics that fit:
- declined by the bank or B-lender: a private mortgage as the bridge;
- self-employed or non-traditional income that banks won't count;
- bruised credit, consumer proposal or past bankruptcy: what a private lender looks at instead (equity, exit plan);
- debt consolidation using home equity when banks say no;
- stopping power of sale, or catching up on arrears: speed matters;
- bridge financing between buying and selling, or closing on time when the bank financing falls through;
- second mortgages and HELOC alternatives;
- the exit plan: private for 1–2 years, then back to a bank or B-lender at renewal. Adrian plans the exit from day one.

## 4. Compliance for private-mortgage sales content (non-negotiable)
Heavy sales never overrides these. The checker enforces them.
- **No "guaranteed approval"**, "everyone approved", "no credit check", "bad credit OK, approved today" or any promise of approval. Use "you may qualify", "subject to lender approval" and "based on equity and exit plan".
- **Be honest about cost:** private mortgages cost more than bank mortgages (higher rates plus lender and broker fees). Any piece that sells private lending says so at least once. A line like "costs more than the bank, short-term by design" works.
- **Any advertised rate** must show the APR and the term (O. Reg. 191/08), be dated and be labelled illustrative. Default: **no rates at all.** Sell speed, approval route and the exit plan instead.
- **No "best rate" or "lowest rate"** claims, no fake testimonials, and no invented client stories. Worked examples are labelled "Illustrative example".
- **Ontario:** brokerage name + licence on every piece (O. Reg. 188/08). Until confirmed, PROOF only.
- **Don't name lenders** or promise timelines as facts. "Can close in days" is allowed only with "in many cases" or "depending on the file".
- All PLAYBOOK banned phrases still apply ("unlock", "game-changer", "dream home", and the rest).
